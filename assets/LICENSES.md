# Art asset licences

Every third-party pack used for SURFBIT art (ARCHITECTURE.md → D14). Only CC0
or explicitly commercial-use licences qualify. Add a row before using a pack.

| Pack | Author | Licence | Source URL | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Coastal Village Lite | Draserszes | Commercial use OK, modify freely, no resale/redistribution, credit not required | https://draserszes.itch.io/coastal-village-topdown-lite | Evaluated at A.3 as a style reference only; nothing from it ships (D14) |

Raw pack downloads go in `assets/packs/<pack-name>/`, which is git-ignored
because the licences forbid redistributing the original files. Only edited,
recolored sprites exported to `public/assets/` are committed.
